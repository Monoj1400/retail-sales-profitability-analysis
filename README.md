# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end retail analytics project using SQL, Power BI, and DAX to analyze sales performance, profitability, discount patterns, product categories, and regional trends.

The objective is to identify the main drivers of profit and loss, understand how discounting relates to profitability, and develop data-driven recommendations for improving business performance.

**Dataset:** Tableau Sample Superstore — a fictitious retail dataset.

## Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL:** Data validation, aggregation, and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX:** KPI and profitability measures
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and type conversion

## Business Questions

1. What are the overall sales, profit, order, and customer metrics?
2. How does sales performance vary throughout the year?
3. Which product categories and subcategories generate the most profit?
4. Which products contribute the greatest losses?
5. How does profitability differ across regions?
6. How are discount levels associated with profit and loss?

## Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately $2.33 million.
- Total profit was approximately $292.30 thousand.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.56%.
- Technology generated the highest category-level profit in the current report.
- Furniture showed comparatively weak overall profitability.
- Tables were a notable loss-making subcategory.
- Higher discount levels were associated with substantial losses in parts of the dataset.

These findings are descriptive and reflect the sample dataset. Discount-related patterns indicate associations, not proof that discounts alone caused the losses.
